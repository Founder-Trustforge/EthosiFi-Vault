Frequently Asked Questions
Is EthosiFi on mainnet yet?
EthosiFi is currently deployed on Ethereum Sepolia testnet with 13 verified contracts. Mainnet launch is targeted for Q3 2026 following completion of our security audit.
Do I need to move my existing funds?
No. EthosiFi wraps your existing wallet — you keep your current address and your existing funds stay where they are. The vault adds a security layer on top.
What is $ETHOS and how do I get it?
$ETHOS is the EthosiFi governance token with a fixed supply of 100 million tokens. It launches with mainnet. Holding 10,000 tokens unlocks permanent Pro access.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes. Cancel anytime — your vault stays active on the Free tier with 10 transactions per month. No cancellation fees, no locked contracts.
Is my private key ever at risk?
Never. EthosiFi is a smart contract security layer — we never have access to your private key, seed phrase, or wallet credentials. The contracts enforce security without accessing your keys.
What chains will EthosiFi support?
We're launching on Ethereum mainnet first. EVM-compatible chains (Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, X1 EcoChain) are on the roadmap based on community demand.
